80. Clicks: in the Panel, click a pill: only the cursor moves there (the Inspector stays); drag across a pill: it selects text; double-click a pill: it selects a word, nothing is copied. Hold ⌘: the pill under the pointer deepens and the pointer is a hand; ⌘-click it: its entry is selected in the Inspector and its text selected in the Input (⌘C copies it). Let go of ⌘: no hand, no deepened pill. The Selection Popover's preview does the same (⌘-click selects the row).
81. Go to Line: in the Panel press ⌘G: a card floats over the top of the Input, "Go to line (1–N), or line:column". Type 120 and Return: the cursor goes to line 120's start, the line scrolls to the middle and flashes yellow, the gutter rings its number. 120:15: its 15th column. 99999: the last line. Letters turn red and Return beeps. Esc or clicking elsewhere closes it. In a Match Window (Tree showing) press ⌘G: it switches to Raw and the card shows over the editor.
82. Shortcuts in Settings: Settings › Shortcuts lists the global shortcut, then each command with its recorder, a ⊗ (no shortcut) and a ↺ (its default). Record ⌃G for Go to Line: the Panel's ⌘G stops working and ⌃G opens the card; the config file has "goToLine": "ctrl+g". Record ⌘E for it: "⌘E is already Toggle Highlighting", the file unchanged; ⌘V: "⌘V is for editing text". Set "Select tab 1–9" to ⌥: ⌥1–⌥9 switch tabs and the tabs' tooltips say so; "Select a Match in the text" to ⌥-click: ⌥ shows the hand. Give Toggle Wrap Lines ⌥⌘W: it toggles wrapping. Restore Defaults puts them all back. Tooltips (Highlighting, Tabs, List) and the menu bar icon's menu (Settings…, Quit) show the shortcuts as set. Settings' General section no longer has "A click on JSON copies"; a config file with "copyJSON" warns that it's ignored.
83. Folding Raw JSON: Pop Out a JSON Match, switch to Raw. Hover the gutter: a ⌄ follows the number of each line an object or array opens on. Click one: the block becomes {⋯}, the numbers skip its lines, the chevron turns ›. Click the ⋯ (the pointer is a hand over it), or the chevron: it opens. Collapse all (the bar's ⇕ icon): every block but the top level folds; Expand all opens them. Fold a block, then type on a line above and below it: it stays folded; ⌘Z undoes the typing, still folded. Put the cursor in the block's text another way (⌘G to a hidden line, ⌘F to text in it): it opens. → onto the ⋯ steps over it. ⌥⌘[ folds the block the cursor is in; ⌥⌘] opens it (both in Settings › Shortcuts). Copy a selection over a fold: all its text is copied. View Original or Beautify: no folds. The Inspector's Raw (Tree | Raw on a JSON card) folds the same, read-only; its search opens the blocks holding what it finds.
84. The popover's scroller: with a mouse attached (or System Settings › Appearance › Show scroll bars: Always), select a long line and press the shortcut: the preview's sideways scroller is the thin one over the text, not a full-size bar under it, and it fades when you stop scrolling.
